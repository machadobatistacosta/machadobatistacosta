<h2 style="border-bottom: 2px solid #9C27B0; padding-bottom: 10px;">My Mission</h2>

<p>
  I am building <b>Apeirum</b> to solve a fundamental crisis: the data sovereignty gap in corporate AI. Most "AI solutions" are data extraction engines for cloud giants. We believe companies should own their context, their latency, and their intelligence—offline, on-prem, and completely secure.
</p>

<ul>
  <li><b>Sovereign Data</b>: Your data never leaves your infrastructure. Perfect for legal, compliance, and heavy industries.</li>
  <li><b>High-Performance Native RAG</b>: Optimized retrieval evaluation. No cloud calls. Zero hallucination architecture.</li>
  <li><b>ptbr-llm</b>: Local models optimized for Brazilian Portuguese grammar, vocabulary, and corporate workflows.</li>
</ul>
